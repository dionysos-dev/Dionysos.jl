486 """

489 Updates `expression` in-place to `expression + (*)(terms...)`.
490
491 This is typically much more efficient than `expression += (*)(terms...)` because

493

495 `expression += a*b`, and `add_to_expression!(expression, a)` produces the same result as
496 `expression += a`.
497

499
