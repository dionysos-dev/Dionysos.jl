Left panel shows non-trivial language inclusion, SAT(Phi_k) and Phi_k implies the supplied reference. Right panel shows the fraction of constructed cumulative conjunctions that are satisfiable. Results are for the task-specific benchmark (4,736 prompts) and VLTL (42 pairs). DeepSeek V4 Pro, T=0.7, R=10 independent samples per prompt. Invalid, disallowed-AP and individually unsatisfiable candidates are discarded; semantic classes are ranked by frequency, with earliest-query tie breaking. Phi_k conjoins min(k,m) representatives. Language-inclusion rates include all prompts in each benchmark denominator. Satisfiability rates condition on at least one retained semantic cluster. Empty candidate pools count as abstentions and are excluded from the satisfiability denominator. Ground-truth references are used as supplied.
